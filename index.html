About Sreeraj

SECURITY ISN'T JUST ABOUT DEFENSE.
IT'S ABOUT UNDERSTANDING HOW SYSTEMS BREAK.

I am a Cybersecurity Researcher and Penetration Tester with a relentless passion for deconstructing complex digital architectures.
